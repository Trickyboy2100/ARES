## Fig1_system_layers

Layered ARES research context. Implemented industrial interfaces are distinguished from synthetic PhysX evidence and proposed method increments. This is not an end-to-end multimodal-policy or real-robot claim.

## Fig2_method_graph

Actual v7 pilot execution graph, not a validated algorithm advantage. A 64D state-based Actor controls bounded XY residuals and a phase-0 contact-offset parameter. B3 directly receives previous observed response/angular motion plus previous action/contact-target commands; B2 has matched zero history channels. The contact-target command is not measured executed displacement; execution must be checked using applied reference and object traces. Standard PPO updates from physical reward. No learned successor-Q head or high-level switch is implemented. The dashed component marks unproven independent benefit, not an absent code path.

## Fig4_historical_online_learning

Historical v6b actual online training. Training bins use completed episodes, not independent test trials. Evaluation uses matched case IDs below 32; source CSVs and hashes identify all runs. Seed envelope is a range, not confidence interval.

## Fig5_historical_frozen_tests

Independent post-step PhysX task acceptance on previously inspected v6b frozen cases. P1 and P2 are separated to avoid masking slot performance. Each task has 16 paired cases; three learned-policy seeds do not create 48 independent scene groups.

## Fig6_new_pilot

New pilot actual completed audited runs only. Development split, bounded training budget, and no imputation of missing methods or seeds. See CSV/source SHA and experimental protocol. Seed dots and error bars show mean plus/minus sample SD, not confidence intervals. B0 is a non-learning controller and has no training seed. Inference masks reuse one seed31 checkpoint; the shared current-state observations remain available, and the partial next-goal mask does not remove the known task direction from the controller. Model training steps are reported per checkpoint and are not summed again for each evaluation row.

## FigS2_new_pilot_online_learning

Actual newly completed P2 training episodes in the corrected formal v7 pilot, binned by 4,096 online environment interactions. Empty bins remain gaps because completed episodes do not necessarily occur in every rollout. Individual seed lines show episode success and return, not a frozen-test learning curve or an uncertainty band. The short 16,384-interaction runs and high nominal-controller performance do not establish convergence or an algorithmic advantage. Pre-correction diagnostic runs outside formal/ are excluded and separately charged to the total research budget.

## FigS3_new_pilot_physical_trajectories

New P2 actual 120 Hz PhysX traces for prespecified diagnostic cases 0 (independently accepted) and 4 (timeout), comparing the same trained B3 seed31 checkpoint with its 8D history inputs masked. Task-frame paths, workpiece angular velocity, geometric paddle/workpiece lateral separation, and time-aligned object trajectory differences are computed from recorded states. The geometric offset is not a measured contact-force signal. Both variants have the same successful case set. Closed-loop differences combine dynamics, policy feedback and numerical sensitivity; they are not an isolated velocity/rotation intervention or evidence of superior success. Raw traces remain non-Git; a 4x-decimated CSV and source hashes are provided.
