\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
The study establishes actual parallel contact-RL training, a limited historical residual-control geometry result, and a correctly connected execution-aware actor objective. It does not establish a superior new manipulation algorithm. R1/R2 effect-history policies do not improve accepted completions; R3's paired three-seed differences are +2/-1/-2 cases and its sample standard deviation is larger, not smaller. The first favorable seed cannot support a robustness claim. All three execution-active successful-case sets are subsets of shared feedback successes: there are no rescues against that controller, only 2/2/8 regressions. Gains in one selected video therefore cannot replace the full comparison.

The score-gradient experiment checks only 512 saved real samples and corresponding initial weights, not a policy-gradient theorem or all contact solver state. The formal seed31 execution-active policy never triggers re-contact and has no valid normal-angle samples, while the ordinary policy has only three. Thus the extended recovery interface is not evidence that recovery or every proposed parameter was learned. Better use of rare legal parameter opportunities, sufficient training coverage and a physically useful parameter intervention must be established before another scaling run. Negative results under the current budget do not disprove contact RL, execution history or marginalization in general.

The v7 near-ceiling pilot, the R1/R2 12 s interfaces and the R3 24 s executor are distinct task versions. Shared footprint repairs, nominal-gain tuning and additional time cannot be attributed to a new RL objective. Repeated development evaluation selects research mechanisms; no sealed-final-set generalization claim is justified. Three seeds and a short pilot budget are insufficient to establish convergence or robust differences. Common-success time excludes failures and is reported separately from all-case cost. Repeated evaluation of the same 32 scenes is not 96 independent scenes.

State-based simulator observations include exact pose and known dynamics context. A deployed perception system, visual/point-cloud actor, measured tool dynamics, true grasping, full robot CCD and real-robot validation have not been demonstrated. The synthetic cube and research paddle are not the field tray. Independent 120 Hz evaluation audits and the separate showcase state sidecar are not complete 120 Hz formal-training observations or a hardware-safety certificate. The re-contact helper avoids the planar object footprint but is not a full robot/rail motion planner. Field references remain private and separate. Long-horizon learned options, VLA instructions and general skill recovery remain extensions, not completed contributions.

The next bounded research decision is to retain the strong controller and ordinary residual policies, test usable contact/re-contact authority on actual failures, and select a parameter interface that changes useful physical outcomes before further method expansion. Event refresh and execution-active likelihood are not promoted on the present evidence. Only a repeatable benefit with mechanism ablations would warrant five seeds, stronger related-method controls and sealed geometry/dynamics tests. The current package is a reproducible research manuscript and client evidence deliverable, not a claim of SCI submission readiness. This narrows the method hypothesis without discarding the platform or the requirement to investigate reinforcement learning.
