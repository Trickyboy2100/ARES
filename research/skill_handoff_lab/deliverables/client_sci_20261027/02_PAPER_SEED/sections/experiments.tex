\section{Experimental Protocol}
\subsection{Physical tasks and evidence levels}
P1 is two-stage free-cube pushing. P2 uses a 40 mm cube, a separate 30 mm research paddle and 100 mm guide rails, with a target sufficiently deep for the cube to enter the guide. It is not precision insertion of a measured industrial tray. Mass, inertia, friction, entrance direction, initial yaw and target vary through actual PhysX properties or geometry. Original robot/gripper collisions remain; the paddle's merged tool inertia retains a historical approximation.

\subsection{Historical splits and v7 evaluation}
Historical v6 uses eight train groups, two validation groups and distinct ID, geometry and dynamics test groups. Three seeds (31, 41, 51) are evaluated on the same 32 cases per split. These historical tests have been read and cannot be reused for selecting new architecture or thresholds. The new pilot uses newly declared development groups, followed by a separately sealed final set only if the mechanism warrants promotion.

In historical v7, B0 is tuned legal damped/task-frame feedback; B1 is BODY-XY residual PPO; B2 is task-frame residual PPO; B3 is the actual effect-history candidate; B4 is finite search or scoring only when the interface supports it. The corrected v7 pilot fixes seeds 31/41/51 and 16,384 online interactions per model, followed by 32 new P2 validation cases with case seed 102741. All learned v7 methods use the same 64-dimensional input shape, two 64-unit hidden layers and three Gaussian outputs. A failed pilot is retained, not replaced by a favorable seed. P2-only completion is the primary metric; P1, time, contact/rotation diagnostics, interaction count and planning budget are secondary.

The preregistered promotion criterion requires a positive B3--B2 P2 count difference in all three paired seeds, mean improvement of at least two cases out of 32, and conditioning interventions with a consistent behavioral/result contribution. Otherwise the mechanism remains a prototype and is not expanded to five seeds or final-test selection. A fixed $-4/0/+4$ mm phase-0 contact-parameter experiment performs three complete physical executions per case; its hindsight union is not an equal-budget online search baseline.

\subsection{Independent process checks and statistics}
Exact post-step terminal state is saved before reset with physics ticks. A 120 Hz sampled FCL/OBB check covers tool, cube, table and rails, plus FK/soft-limit and final stability checks. A separate mesh diagnostic samples actual robot collision geometry in a limited subset. Neither is continuous-collision detection or a hardware safety certificate. A simulated 80 N net-contact bound is uncalibrated for hardware.

We report per-seed counts and paired case differences. Seed sample standard deviation is not a binomial confidence interval, and repeated evaluation of the same cases under multiple models does not create independent environments. Time comparisons use explicitly identified common-success cases. Additional prediction/suffix interaction, demonstration continuation and diagnostic runs are accounted for separately. Software-incomplete runs are not assigned fabricated physical failure labels.
\subsection{Subsequent closed-loop task versions and logging}
The continuation uses explicitly versioned interfaces: R1 is 64D/3D with phase1 contact-target control; R2 is 66D/4D after shared rotated-contact geometry repair and finite tool yaw; formal R3 is 87D/5D after adding observable controller memory and legal re-contact. R1/R2 use a 12 s deadline, while every formal R3 comparator uses 24 s. Success thresholds are not relaxed. These task-version and horizon changes prevent attributing a cross-round count difference to the likelihood objective.

The R3 plan fixes paired seeds 31/41/51 for event-refresh ordinary and execution-active PPO, 32,768 interactions per model, plus one seed31 fixed-refresh run per objective. Completed rows and their checkpoint-linked budgets are generated from the actual run index; a scheduled but incomplete run is not evidence. R1/R2 use one seed per method and cannot support a cross-seed claim. Development cases are paired within a task version, not independent repetitions when evaluated under several seeds. Sealed final scenarios remain unopened.

Formal training saves complete 60 Hz pre-action observation/action records and episode post-step states. Its 120 Hz process traces are evaluation traces, not complete 120 Hz formal-training logs. The additional live-training showcase separately records a 44-field state sidecar at 120 Hz; it is explicitly not the complete 87D pre-action observation at that frequency. Camera re-evaluations and physical probes incur evaluation cost but no training interactions. The showcase has its own checkpoint and counted training interactions, including a failed first recording attempt; it cannot silently replace a formal model trained under the fixed comparison budget. The public package contains only synthetic task evidence, not field records.
