# Prior-art boundaries, checked against primary material

No mechanism below is claimed novel merely because it is implemented in Peg.

- **Option-Critic**: learns intra-option policies and termination; source explicitly keeps the current option until termination and mixes continuation/switch values. Our event termination and frozen intra-option actor are simpler fixed contracts, not a reproduction of learned Option-Critic. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05140), [actual network source](https://github.com/jeanharb/option_critic/blob/5d6c81a650a8f452bc8ad3250f1f211d317fde8c/neural_net.py). Reviewed execution loop in train_agent.py and actor/termination/Q equations. Revision5d6c81a650a8f452bc8ad3250f1f211d317fde8c.
- **RecoveryChaining**: nominal/recovery skill composition and switching already exist. Its SkillChain accumulates duration-discounted skill returns; RecoverySkillDRL executes until its wrapped environment terminates the option. Our reference continuation is not a new recovery-switch idea, and we have not reproduced its benchmark. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13979), [skill chain source](https://github.com/shivamvats/recovery-chaining/blob/4894acc3768678de4dee6358df14c461a411162e/belief_srs/skills/skill_chain.py), [recovery training source](https://github.com/shivamvats/recovery-chaining/blob/4894acc3768678de4dee6358df14c461a411162e/belief_srs/skills/recovery_skill.py).
- **BCP / Continue or Replan (2026)**: frozen base VLA, learned ordinal Bernoulli continuation, trajectory-level GRPO with replanning-efficiency reward. It already learns when to replan. We instead study measured paired outcomes under a declared physical Option continuation; this difference is a research setting, not established originality. [Paper method](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03483), [official project](https://fleetfootwork.github.io/BCP/). The project page's Code label exposed no active source link during inspection; no claim that BCP implementation was reproduced.
- **ACPPO-Corr (2026)**: chunked planner plus stepwise corrector, chunked likelihood ratios and separate updates. Actual acppo_res.py contains planner/corrector freezing, joint action likelihood and masked KL. Our joint suffix-likelihood prototype therefore cannot claim novelty for coupling temporal actions in an update. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36250), [official implementation](https://github.com/hshhahn/ACPPO/blob/1007a44b2dec5529bf6c2a92acd91627d4f4a5bf/isaacgymenvs/rl_games/algos_torch/acppo_res.py). No external IsaacGym installation or benchmark execution.
- **Commit While Futures Agree (2026)**: frozen policy/world model, common-noise imagined futures, consequence disagreement and BIC-based commitment. Our work uses measured native reexecutions, not predicted future consensus, and adds actual Actor updates. This distinction does not imply a superior method. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07949).
- **SPIBB**: baseline bootstrapping under uncertain support is established. Its tabular source builds count-based masks and constrains policy mass. Our suffix KL is not its safety guarantee. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.06924), [source inspected](https://github.com/RomainLaroche/SPIBB/blob/master/spibb.py).
- **AWAC / Q-filter / Policy Decorator** remain inherited mature comparisons: advantage-weighted imitation, critic-filtered demonstrations, and controlled residual exploration must not be renamed. [AWAC paper/code](https://awacrl.github.io/), [Q-filter paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.10089), [Policy Decorator paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13630). The current target replan replaces a joint path and is not a bounded motor residual. The present comparison adds same-data success BC and local reference-KL, not a full reimplementation of every cited algorithm.

Research conclusion: explicit continuation and proper SMDP boundaries are necessary implementation assets. Local preference+KL, joint trajectory preference, and return-at-termination correction have mature antecedents. None has demonstrated independent developmental gain here. The new evidence isolates local fitting versus state/timing generalization; it does not justify a new algorithm name or monotonic-improvement theorem.
