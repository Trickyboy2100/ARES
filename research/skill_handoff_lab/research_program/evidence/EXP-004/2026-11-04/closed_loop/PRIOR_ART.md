Candidate: estimate a discrete completion-time hazard from real current suffix trajectories, right-censor failures at their actual remaining budget, and use its CDF at the actual handoff deadline to update predecessor action values. Ordinary SAC and scalar suffix-value controls get identical physical states/actions/rewards/data opportunity and fit the same auxiliary capacity; only candidate/explicit suffix comparator uses those labels in predecessor backups. All policies still observe budget. A budget-blind head ablation is available; no independent novelty or gain is asserted.

Important competing prior: Censoring-Aware Time-to-Success Potential Shaping already uses censored discrete hazards in RL (https://www.mdpi.com/2073-431X/15/10/686). Our current candidate changes predecessor policy-iteration targets rather than rewards, but that difference alone is not a publishable contribution.
