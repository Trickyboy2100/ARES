# Source-level method comparison and claim boundaries

The downloaded originals are read locally, never executed as unreviewed scripts or redistributed here. `prior_code/INDEX.json` pins commit, file and SHA. Existing STAP, DAWN and SeqDex pins remain in the previous peg_value package. This is public simulated research only.

## Mature V0/V1 foundation

- [IQL](https://github.com/ikostrikov/implicit_q_learning): inspected `critic.py`, `actor.py`, `learner.py`, commit `09d700248117881a75cb21f0adb95c6c8a694cb2`. Dataset-action Q, asymmetric expectile V and exp-advantage weighted behavioral cloning avoid continuous OOD action maximization. Our categorical IQL uses expectile .7, inverse temperature 3, weight cap 100. This is a finite-action adaptation, not a reproduction of D4RL results.
- [RLPD](https://github.com/ikostrikov/rlpd): inspected `train_finetuning.py` and `sac_learner.py`, commit `c90fd4baf28c9c9ef40a81460a2e395092844f88`. Its 50:50 prior/online replay, critic normalization, ensembles and high critic UTD are existing techniques. We inherit 50:50 sampling and critic LayerNorm; use two critics, categorical exact expectation, fixed alpha .02, 50 actor+critic updates per full physical episode. This is NOT full RLPD (its critic/actor schedule and ensembles differ). Budget is observed; gamma=1 matches terminal native success, with duration retained explicitly.
- [TD3+BC](https://github.com/sfujim/TD3_BC): inspected `TD3_BC.py`, commit `8791ad7d7656cb8396f1b3ac8c37f170b2a2dd5f`: deterministic continuous actor, smoothed target actions and Q-normalized BC penalty. These continuous smoothing operations do not directly respect our 3/2 physical options. We do not label categorical IQL or CQL as TD3+BC.
- [DAWN](https://github.com/Guozheng-Ma/DAWN): re-read prior pinned warmup, hard/soft Q targets, residual actor and LayerNorm critic. Warmup and normalization are baseline engineering. The normalized continuous projected SAC control matches prior data, width and optimizer budget to reduce the normalization confound. Discrete output heads necessarily have different sizes; no exact parameter-count match is claimed.
- [STAP](https://github.com/agiachris/STAP): retain the already-tested official algorithm kernel and log-Q repairs. Current functional finite-action comparator learns predecessor reach Q, actual successor dynamics and successor Q, then enumerates all three first options and two successor options. No official benchmark or full CEM retraining claim. Its additional dynamics computation is recorded separately.
- [SeqDex](https://github.com/sequential-dexterity/SeqDex): re-read terminal success/failure balancing and transition feasibility training. Real successor feasibility and backward value propagation are prior art. Conditional absolute supervision is a direct control, not a novel method.

## Acquisition: what is existing, what is actually hypothesized

- [EES / Practice Makes Perfect](https://ees.csail.mit.edu/), official release `planning-to-practice-ees`, commit `5c5307e4dc4bad9102cfd9955648df5ec42e8c9d`: inspected `competence_models.py`, active sampler approach and explorer. EES estimates competence, extrapolates improvement from practice, and recomputes competence-aware task plans. Its optimistic model uses recent empirical competence changes; the full latent model fits monotonic beta regression with EM. `practice` in our first loop is only a local uncertainty/headroom proxy, not EES. The stronger `ees_pair` comparator explicitly uses empirical competence extrapolation and predecessor-plan improvement on the same executable prefix pool. It is a finite two-option functional analogue, not a full symbolic EES reproduction or the latent-beta model.
- [SUNG](https://github.com/guosyjlu/SUNG), commit `0f0113fa8951635e2e459173eb048e57d335df6e`: inspected TD3 exploration and conservative SAC/VAE. SUNG combines value and VAE state-action density uncertainty, and adaptively applies conservative objectives. Our twin-Q uncertainty acquisition is a functional UQ baseline, not SUNG itself. No extra density network is smuggled into the candidate.
- [SPIBB](https://github.com/RomainLaroche/SPIBB), commit `833f32b642759111907d2c4d086d1ae1ce9d84cb`: count-based uncertain state-action mask and constrained policy improvement retain baseline mass where unsupported. A nominal fallback alone is prior art. No SPIBB finite-state safety guarantee is asserted for neural continuous observations.
- [RecoveryChaining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13979): read original paper (hybrid recovery/nominal options and learning initiation/handover). No official source repository was located in this search; source reproduction is NOT claimed. Our task has two fixed legal option sets, without learning a low-level recovery controller or changing physical permissions.

## Single candidate developed this round

Initial advantage-uncertainty acquisition has a structural weakness: with two actions the incumbent advantage variance is identically zero, so its score tends to select the other action. Multiplying all successor scores by the same remaining budget cannot alter their ranking. These controls are retained; they are not a proposed contribution.

The revised `handoff_update` candidate changes only WHICH measured physical prefix to practice next. From an actually observed handoff and its predecessor record, enumerate the four possible terminal label pairs. For each hypothetical pair, make one virtual terminal-Q SGD step, then one virtual predecessor Bellman step; score expected regret of retaining the current initial-option decision versus reselecting under that updated critic on training initial-state anchors. Every chosen pair executes two complete native100 episodes from legal reset, with the identical real prefix checked afterwards. No hidden prefix restoration, future-label selection or uncharged simulator query.

This differs operationally from local competence extrapolation and raw Q uncertainty: it asks whether a successor observation would change predecessor deployment decisions through the learner's update. It is also related to established expected model change / value-of-information acquisition; novelty is NOT established by this implementation. `local_update` removes predecessor Bellman propagation while retaining two virtual updates; random-pair and EES-style controls share pool, data privileges, optimizer and full physical cost.

Limitations: virtual SGD (.01) approximates the real Adam update; branch probabilities are clipped current-Q plug-in independent Bernoulli factors, NOT a calibrated posterior (soft Q is not exactly a probability). Scores use one critic while deployment uses the ordinary trained actor. A failed acquisition score can therefore reflect this approximation rather than disprove all task-conditioned active learning. No auxiliary head, modified reward, future observation or old failed paired-value loss is used.

## Evidence-driven revisions within the same session

The first same-prefix pool experiment repeatedly acquired equal-outcome pairs and did not improve deployment. The next revision shares a coverage constraint with all controls and allows querying previously unexecuted predecessor offsets; the controller, learner objective and per-pair full200-step cost stay fixed. A normal stochastic SAC collector uses the same8 conditions, same initial8 warmup episodes, same24 full-episode budget and same2000 updates as a separate matched control. The128-episode/64-condition ordinary SAC scale check is explicitly a DIFFERENT budget/distribution, not an equal-budget candidate comparison.

A further falsification isolates the candidate's independent-label assumption. Scene-held-out old real pairs show conditional-label calibration errors when shared physical failure is ignored. A five-neighbor Dirichlet joint-outcome table and an independence table with EXACTLY THE SAME MARGINALS are compared. Both are frozen from the same29 prior handoff contexts; neither changes the critic loss, rewards or observations. A shadow run first verifies whether the probability change changes acquisition choices, then both variants execute actual full-prefix physics. Prediction calibration is never called RL progress. This remains the same expected-update acquisition hypothesis with a branch-probability ablation, not a renamed old paired-value auxiliary.

The new finite STAP comparator was initially evaluated with probability products. The inherited log-Q rule was restored; all24 directly measured STAP decisions remain identical, and an explicit underflow adversary verifies the repair (`STAP_LOGQ_AUDIT.json`). Original source snapshots and physical data are retained.

Stochastic ordinary SAC uses its own learner generator for both action sampling and minibatches. Exact optimizer reconstruction therefore includes policy sampling RNG consumption. Same seed alone does not mean identical minibatch streams across stochastic-collector and deterministic-allocator families. Primary paired/frontier ablations share a deterministic action-selection path and common learning initialization. Raw action/buffer records and optimizer replay checks expose this distinction.
