## Algorithm implementation and three actual research loops

1. Scene-group cross-validation on the existing 190 Tool episodes (23 scenes, 38 grasp contexts; 26 reach the postcontact decision). Ordinary two-stage Fitted Q, matched-pair consistency, shuffled/unpaired control, direct supervised outcome, mean-suffix-value analogue and discrete STAP-style Q-product/dynamics were trained, 600 updates, three training seeds and three scene folds. Terminal critic inputs are identical across baselines; FQI/supervised/foresight differ in predecessor targets, not six independent successor models. Paired consistency improves ranking only 162/180 to 163/180 repeated discordant comparisons, with unchanged selected success. No gain.
2. Competitive explanation: equal-outcome pairs dilute the learning signal. Training only discordant differences makes joint selection worse (34/69 versus ordinary 36/69); equally weighted absolute mixed-label training reaches 36/69. This excludes that sampling change as an improvement on this dataset.
3. Integrate the SAME contrast loss into inherited masked LN-SAC critic gradients, without adding a head. Actor/critic update counts and capacity are matched; all-data auxiliary backprop has extra computation, disclosed in TRAINING.json. At 200 updates, logged closed-candidate whole-chain selection is ordinary32/69, paired40/69, absolute38/69, shuffled39/69. At1200: ordinary44/69, paired43/69, absolute44/69, shuffled42/69. These are 23 scene groups repeated across three train seeds, not69 independent scenes. Same data and closed candidate projections apply to all; this is not fresh physics or proof of sample efficiency. The shuffled RNG caveat is in PRIOR_ART_COMPARISON.md.

The prototype was then reused on the actual Peg two-option data. Scene-group FQI: ordinary18/30 versus paired16/30 joint selections (10 scenes ×3 train seeds). This also does not support independent gain. Both stages have one effective scalar action. Peg SAC explicitly masks the unused second coordinate in BOTH critic and entropy, rather than transferring the Tool two-dimensional successor entropy. Runtime projects grasp to the same three legal offsets and insertion to the same two target choices for every learned method. Full-data Peg SAC has118 real option transitions from60 physical episodes; this is a working bounded baseline, not sufficient convergence evidence.

## Research decision

RETAINED: GPU OBB adaptation, strong common Peg executor, actual two-option SkillResult interface, exact-prefix comparisons, ordinary masked SAC and the minimal critic-contrast implementation with controls. LOCAL_NEGATIVE: matched return differences, focusing discordant pairs, and their SAC auxiliary do not yield independent task gains. OPEN_MECHANISM: naturally varying Peg conditions produce different useful grasp/handoff choices, while the remaining failure includes contact tracking and finite budget despite correct target semantics. Ordinary RL is operational but not declared sufficiently converged from118 Peg transitions.

Next experiment should distinguish an executable contact correction from a purely predicted successor advantage on the common Peg controller: measure actual contact/axial progress and remaining budget, then compare a bounded reapproach/hold primitive against a strong shared geometry/feedback baseline. Keep the same critic prototype for a controlled continuation if new paired data genuinely change decision value; do not restart a collection of renamed auxiliary heads or treat the old closed-set early advantage as novelty. The primary paper question remains task-conditioned multi-stage executable skill RL. EXP-003 shared-baseline and EXP-004 method work continue together. No independent contribution is established yet.
