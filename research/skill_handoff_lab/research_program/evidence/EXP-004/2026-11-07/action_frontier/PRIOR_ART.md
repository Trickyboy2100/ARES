# Executable action coverage and policy coordinates: prior art

This is a candidate representation/learning study, not a claim that a full action catalogue, SAC, factorization, supervised scoring or gradual exploration is a new algorithm.

- **PEARL**, AAAI2026, [primary paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39635), [official code](https://github.com/AAIR-lab/PEARL), inspected commit `7c242fc9b5d57e1d4a75e071316b21c4285e72c9`: `src/agents/pearl.py` alternates TD learning with state/action abstraction refinement using TD-error and value buffers; refinement can depend on evaluation success. Our fixed executable catalogue and fixed full-rank Actor coordinates are not its learned abstraction tree. We do not call catalogue expansion an original abstraction algorithm. No top-level license was found; code was inspected only, not copied or executed.
- **ViSkill**, [Value-Informed Skill Chaining, IROS2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16503), [official code](https://github.com/med-air/ViSkill), inspected commit `99b8edd2a2c51ad85ef023bf8ddcf9a433bc76f5`: `viskill/agents/sc_sac.py` explicitly bootstraps predecessor values through next-subtask policy/critic and supports a pretrained last subtask. Our actual grasp-to-SkillResult transition and current-policy SMDP backup inherit that established principle. This is the surgical-robot ViSkill, not the unrelated2026 VLM project with the same name. Repository LICENSE retained locally; no code imported.
- **Policy Decorator**, [official code](https://github.com/tongzhoumu/policy_decorator): inspected previously pinned `policy_decorator.py`, especially lines430–446. It ramps probability of enabling residual actions and adds scaled motor residuals to base actions. We reuse the mature exploration principle for categorical Options; our actions are planner/execution Options, not bounded motor residuals. This is a functional control ingredient, not official benchmark replication.
- **Action Branching**, [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08946), and **MP-DQN**, [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04388): parameterized/branched action representations precede this work. Our flat versus reference/offset Actor coordinate comparison has identical observable input, Critic, action authority, parameter count, and full logit expressivity. A positive result would first be evidence for this physical inductive bias, not proof of general algorithmic novelty.
- STAP's downstream feasibility and RecoveryChaining's nominal/recovery handback remain strong conceptual precedents; their source reviews and pinned hashes are retained in the previous `joint_continuation/PRIOR_ART.md`. No claim of official-task leaderboard superiority is made here.

## Concrete hypothesis and falsification

The finite design frontier contains physical cases where translating the current requested target fails but refreshing the measured attachment/reference and applying the same signed offset succeeds; other states exhibit the opposite hazard. The Actor must therefore select **reference and offset jointly**, not collapse them as the same3mm action. A shared reference/offset logit basis with reference-specific interaction coordinates may transfer useful gradient information while retaining distinct choices. It is a fixed, full-rank coordinate reparameterization, not a new source of observations, query access or extra expressivity. Equal final success, added regressions, or benefits explained by ordinary full-catalogue SAC reject an independent candidate advantage.

The classifier comparator trains selected-action terminal outcome scores on both positive and negative own-policy trajectories, using the same initial logits and design data. Its suffix labels are behavior-policy outcomes; it has no claim to exact current-policy Q. No previous MC-mix, alias-Critic or paired-PG loss is expanded.

## Revision: state-conditioned action relations are also established prior art

[AGILE: Know Your Action Set, ICLR2022](https://github.com/clvrai/agile), inspected commit `7eeb7b7ac248f98814aadb4f643a70d4eafc3e9f`, joins state and action representations, applies graph attention, and produces categorical action utilities. Inspected `policy_based/method/models/main_method.py` (GraphCategorical), `gnn/action_graph.py` and `gnn/GAT/layers.py`. This directly precedes the broad idea of state-conditioned action relations. Our revised fixed target-geometry kernel is not a new general action-relation architecture; it supplies a specific executable-reference bias without a learned graph, decoder or extra simulator queries. No top-level license found; source inspected only, not imported/copied. No official AGILE benchmark superiority is claimed.

[Learning Action Representations, ICML2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/chandak19a.html) decomposes policies through an action representation and mapping back to executable actions. [HyAR, ICLR2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05490) uses a learned hybrid representation and decoder with dynamics-informed smoothness. These precedents rule out calling embedding or sharing between related actions itself original. The present revision keeps the finite legal actions and current executor, and computes similarity from already observable requested-target geometry. A static-reference ablation is needed if the state-conditioned version shows benefit. General relational-policy and learned-embedding comparisons remain unrun, so novelty and broad superiority remain unestablished.


## Policy-extraction follow-up: do not mislabel the Mean-Q control as VIAC

[Value Improved Actor Critic, NeurIPS2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/4a2228d3db1135f13705b0b5844c6708-Abstract-Conference.html), [official source](https://github.com/YanivO1123/viac), commit `1b0b0b89c6674f8a2e5ba6f7acea0bf70ba32f3a`, inspected `vi_sac_continuous_action.py:394–446`. The released SAC variant changes Critic training with an asymmetric expectile loss while retaining sampled Actor target actions and the ordinary Actor objective. The paper distinguishes value improvement from changing the acting policy. Our frozen greedy Mean-Q deployment ablation is neither a VIAC implementation nor a refutation of VIAC. It changes behavior without retraining the evaluated value; the observed failures motivate separating continuation/value learning from extraction. No top-level license found; inspected only, not copied/executed. New training against this mature alternative remains unrun.
