# ARES repository agent guidance

When working on the client SCI research under `research/skill_handoff_lab/`, first read
[`research/skill_handoff_lab/research_program/README.md`](research/skill_handoff_lab/research_program/README.md),
then its `STATE.json` and the active experiment card. The scoped
`research/skill_handoff_lab/AGENTS.md` provides additional research rules.

The project is a real research program, not a queue of disconnected prompts.
Never replace prior experimental evidence to make a result positive; use the single
paper question, evidence ledger and next experiment to guide work.

For tasks outside `research/skill_handoff_lab/`, this research-program guidance
does not redefine their scope. Do not treat the program as authorization for
robot motion, production changes, restricted field-data disclosure, or killing
unrelated processes.
